- **A resumed chunked export, or a chunk worker claiming its next chunk, could fail with `database is locked` beside another writer of the same SQLite state file in the same process (a replan, a claim, or a write by a sibling worker).** The in-process write queue let only one writer that had waited past its patience keep its place; a second such writer fell back to SQLite's unqueued busy wait and lost every gap to a sibling committing back to back until the 10 s busy timeout, and the chunk claim never joined the queue at all. **Now** every writer that waited past its patience keeps its place, and the chunk claim takes its turn like any other write, so a replan or claim beside another writer waits for its turn and lands. **What to do:** nothing; state files and exit codes are unchanged.
