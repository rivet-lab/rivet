- **`validate --depth full` failed a healthy split prefix, and a foreign export could tile a gap in a `--pool --split` range.** The value-checksum read of split unit manifests counted a `Failed` attempt, so a failed attempt superseded and gc'd made `rivet validate --depth full` exit 1 ("could not be verified") on a prefix whose successes were whole; the completed-unit tiling in `--pool --split` took manifests of any export family, so a foreign export sharing the prefix gave a false refusal or let a `_SUCCESS` stand over a gap. **Now** every reader of the run manifests under a prefix (the value-checksum re-read, the CDC position check, validate's sibling merge and the split tiling) goes through one census that keeps only `Success` runs of the export's own family. **Behaviour change:** a prefix that held a failed attempt's manifest used to fail `validate --depth full`; it now passes, and a split prefix shared with another export no longer counts that export's units. Nothing is written or migrated: manifests and tables written before the upgrade are read as before, and no config change is needed.
