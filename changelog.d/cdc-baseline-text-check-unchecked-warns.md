- **A CDC baseline leg admitted without its text-declaration check now says so.** On a PostgreSQL source, `rivet run`, `check` and `doctor` refuse a baseline leg that declares `text` under `columns:` over a date, time, timestamp or array column, because the change stream renders that cell in its own form (see the refusal added with the PostgreSQL text remedy). When rivet could not connect to the source, or the catalog describe failed, the leg was admitted with no word. Now it is still admitted, with the same exit code, and a WARN names the export, the step that failed (`connect` or `describe`), the credential-masked cause, and that the check did not run. When the caller already holds a source connection, the check reads on it instead of opening a second one. A source that is reachable, a leg that declares no `text` column and a non-PostgreSQL source are unchanged and print nothing new. Nothing written before the upgrade changes. **What to do:** none for a WARN; restore the source connection and re-run `rivet check` to get the refusal where one applies.
