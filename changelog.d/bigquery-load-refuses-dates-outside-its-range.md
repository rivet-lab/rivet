- **A BigQuery load of a date or timestamp outside BigQuery's range is refused by rivet before any write, as `RIVET_LOAD_VALUE_OUT_OF_TARGET_RANGE` (exit 5).** Behaviour change. BigQuery holds `0001-01-01` to `9999-12-31` in `DATE`, `DATETIME` and `TIMESTAMP`, and fails the whole load job on a value outside it. Before, rivet staged the part and BigQuery rejected the job (`RIVET_LOAD_WAREHOUSE_REJECTED`), for example a MySQL `DATE '0000-01-01'`. **Now** `rivet load` reads the footer statistics of each staged part of a plan holding a date or timestamp column and stops before the write, naming the column, the value and the range, in every load mode. The check applies to BigQuery only: Snowflake delivers such years as text forms and ClickHouse keeps its own check. A part whose footer holds no statistics is not refused for BigQuery, which fails such a load itself. Tables and state written before the upgrade are untouched: a config that loaded is not refused, one BigQuery rejected is now refused earlier by a coded stop and a partitioned BigQuery load reads each footer twice. **What to do:** cast the column to text in the export's `query:` to keep the value, or correct it at the source, then re-export.
