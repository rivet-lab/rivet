- **Behaviour change**: a SQL Server `time` column of scale 7 (the bare `time` default) is delivered as a nanosecond time of day (`Time64(ns)`, ClickHouse `Decimal(18, 9)`, BigQuery INT64 nanoseconds of day, Snowflake recovered with `TIME_FROM_PARTS`) on batch and on CDC, so the 7th digit (the 100 ns tick) is kept instead of dropped to microseconds. `time(0..6)` is unchanged. A CDC value with a 100 ns digit into a microsecond column is refused instead of truncated. Parts and warehouse tables written before the upgrade keep `Time64(µs)` (ClickHouse `Decimal(18, 6)`): the first run after it reports the column once as `type changed: <column> (Time64(Microsecond) → Time64(Nanosecond))` under the default `on_schema_drift: warn`, and stops under `on_schema_drift: fail`. **What to do:** under `on_schema_drift: fail`, run once under `warn`; a warehouse table that already holds the microsecond column is loaded into a new table, or dropped and recreated; to keep the microsecond landing, cast the column in the export query (`CAST(<column> AS time(6))`).
